Compass — clickable prototypes

Lo-fi wireframes for Compass, a tool that maps untagged non-production AWS resources to the product line that owns them (cost attribution, showback & self-serve tagging). Deliberately low-fidelity — grayscale structure with the four confidence bands carried in color.
